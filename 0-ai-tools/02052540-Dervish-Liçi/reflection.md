# Personal Reflection

## What surprised me most?

Going into this research, I already had some general knowledge about AI coding tools, so nothing came as a major surprise. However, the research helped me better understand the depth and impact of these tools on the software engineering industry, confirming and strengthening what I already believed.

## What concerns me about AI in software engineering?

I am not scared of AI taking over human jobs — I genuinely do not think that will ever happen in the software engineering industry. However, what does concern me is the impact AI is having on junior developers and students like myself. It is becoming increasingly harder to find internships and entry level jobs, as companies can now do more with fewer people. This is a real challenge that the industry needs to address.
