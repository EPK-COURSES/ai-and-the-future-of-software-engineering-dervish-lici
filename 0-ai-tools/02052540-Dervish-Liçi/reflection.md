# Personal Reflection

## What surprised me most?

Going into this research, I already had some general knowledge about AI coding tools, so nothing came as a major surprise. However, the research helped me better understand the depth and impact of these tools on the software engineering industry, confirming and strengthening what I already believed.

## What concerns me about AI in software engineering?

I am not scared of AI taking over human jobs — I genuinely do not think that will ever happen in the software engineering industry. However, what does concern me is the impact AI is having on junior developers and students like myself. It is becoming increasingly harder to find internships and entry level jobs, as companies can now do more with fewer people. This is a real challenge that the industry needs to address.

## What opportunities do I see?

I see AI as a massive opportunity rather than a threat. As a future software engineer, I plan to use AI tools to write code faster, catch errors more efficiently, and save time on repetitive tasks. That saved time can be reinvested into more productive and meaningful work — whether that is improving my skills, solving bigger problems, or contributing more value to a company. People who are not well informed tend to fear AI, but I believe those who embrace it will have a significant advantage.

## How will I personally adapt?

This research made me even more convinced that AI will not replace humans but instead create new job opportunities that require more productive and creative thinking. My opinion shifted from being generally positive about AI to believing that AI tools should not just be optional — they should be a standard part of every software engineer's workflow. I plan to stay informed, keep learning, and treat AI as a powerful partner rather than a threat.
